2. Customer Segments

 1. All Vehicle Owners: Commercial fleet operators, logistics companies, and everyday diesel
 vehicle owners face constant struggles with strict urban emission norms, frequent DPF
 clogging during stop-and-go traffic, and expensive OEM replacement cycles. AeroPure
 addresses this by offering a cost-effective, modular aftermarket solution featuring quick-
 disconnect couplings and real-time dashboard monitoring that allows for rapid, hazard-free
 servicing and minimized vehicle downtime.


 2. Small & Medium Enterprises (MSME) Industries: Small-scale manufacturing units,
 commercial complexes, and workshops relying heavily on diesel backup generators or
 localized industrial exhaust equipment require affordable ways to comply with pollution
 control board regulations. AeroPure provides these MSMEs with a compact, low-
 backpressure, multi-stage filtration system that delivers efficient particulate scrubbing and
 thermal management without the massive footprint or water consumption of traditional
 industrial wet scrubbers.
3. Unique Value Proposition (USP)
 Key Benefits
1.Preventing Pollution at a Very Low Cost: Designed with affordable, commercially off-the-shelf (COTS) components and
replaceable modular layers.
2.inimized Engine Strain & Low Backpressure: Unlike conventional static filters that trap soot and choke the engine,
AeroPure's integrated thermal management (heat sinks and fluid cooling) and graded pore structure maintain smooth
exhaust flow to protect engine efficiency and save fuel.
3.Rapid, Hazard-Free Modular Servicing:Equipped with self-sealing docking mechanisms and quick-disconnect couplings,
the system allows operators or technicians to swap filter modules in minutes without toxic exposure or complicated
teardowns.

Differentiators:
1.Effortless Government Norms & Compliance: Built to proactively meet strict urban Pollution Control Board (PCB) and
periodic vehicle emission test (PUC) standards, helping both vehicle owners and MSME generators avoid hefty fines and legal
shutdowns.
2.Multi-Stage Active Thermal & Nano-Filtration: Combines oil-cooled heat sinks, nano-engineered charcoal matrices, and
synthetic arrays into a single compact unit outperforming single-stage DPFs that frequently fail under heavy thermal stress.
3.Real-Time Digital Monitoring: Unlike traditional passive exhaust pipes that operate blindly, AeroPure integrates live sensor
tracking and dashboard alerts to notify users of filter saturation before performance drops.

Tagline:
Modular Filtration, Zero Compromise: Smart Exhaust Purification for Vehicles and MSME.

 5. Solution
Product Overview:

1.Multi-Stage Membrane & Nano-Filtration: Combines oil-cooled heat sinks, nano-engineered charcoal matrices, and graded
synthetic arrays into a compact unit to capture fine particulate matter effectively.
2.Real-Time Sensor Diagnostics: Integrates continuous particulate and temperature monitoring to track filter saturation
levels and alert users before efficiency drops.
3.Modular Service Architecture: Built with quick-disconnect couplings and self-sealing docking mechanisms that allow
individual filter layers to be swapped out rapidly and safely.

Problem–Solution Fit:

1.Easily Fitted & Portable: Designed as a compact, retrofit unit that can be easily installed on diverse vehicle exhaust tailpipes
and small MSME generator stacks without altering core engine architecture.
2.Eliminates Urban Clogging & Backpressure: Solves the frequent urban stop-and-go clogging issue of conventional DPFs by
utilizing optimized thermal management and low-backpressure graded pores to protect engine performance.
3.Affordable Maintenance vs. Expensive Replacements: Replaces costly OEM filter overhauls with inexpensive, modular
replacement layers that fit a student-friendly or small-business budget.

10. Unfair Advantage

Unfair Advantage & Competitive Moat
Unique Strategic Assets:

1.Proprietary Modular Architecture: The self-sealing docking mechanism and quick-disconnect coupling design
allow for instant, tool-free layer swaps, completely bypassing the welded, non-serviceable limitations of
conventional exhaust filters.

2.Integrated Dual-Phase Thermal & Nano-Filtration: Combining oil-cooled heat sinks with a multi-layered
nano-charcoal and graded synthetic array into a single compact unit delivers superior particulate capture
without high backpressure.

Long-Term Defensibility:

1.Low-Cost Scalability: Built using affordable Commercial Off-The-Shelf (COTS) materials, creating high entry
barriers for competitors trying to match our low production and replacement costs.

2.High Switching Costs & Recurring Ecosystem: Once vehicle fleets and MSME operators adopt the modular
housing, they remain locked into purchasing proprietary, low-cost consumable filter cartridge replacements.
